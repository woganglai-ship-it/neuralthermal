实验室访问隐私说明

我们保存您提交的姓名、公司或机构、邮箱、关注方向、同意选项，以及实验室访问时间和模块名称，用于提供访问、了解需求和处理咨询。不记录您在实验室中输入的仿真参数。

访问凭证通过有效期 30 天的必要 Cookie 保存在当前浏览器。为防止滥用，提交频率通过短期哈希标识限制，不将原始 IP 加入客户档案。

资料通过 Cloudflare 基础设施处理，客户档案仅限登录后的团队工作台查看。只有您另行勾选同意，我们才会主动就相关技术需求联系您。不会自动发送营销邮件。

如需查询、更正、删除资料或撤回联系同意，请邮件至 tech@neuralthermal.com，注明提交时使用的邮箱。我们在处理请求时可能需要核实邮箱归属。资料会保留至需求处理完成或您要求删除；与持续合作有关的记录按实际需要保留。

Lab access privacy

We store your name, organization, email, area of interest, consent choices, and lab visit times and module names to provide access and handle inquiries. Simulation inputs are not recorded. A necessary cookie remembers access for 30 days. Short-lived hashed identifiers help limit abuse; raw IP addresses are not added to lead profiles.

Data is processed using Cloudflare infrastructure. Lead profiles are available only to authenticated team members. Proactive technical follow-up requires your separate opt-in; marketing emails are not sent automatically. Contact tech@neuralthermal.com to access, correct or delete your details, or withdraw follow-up consent. We may verify email ownership. Records are retained until the inquiry is resolved or deletion is requested; ongoing collaboration records are retained as needed.
